📖 Understanding Percentile Lines
  • P3 — 3rd percentile. Only 3% of children weigh/measure less. Values below this may indicate underweight or short stature and should be discussed with a pediatrician.
  • P15 — 15th percentile. 15% of children are below this line. Still within normal range but on the lower side.
  • P50 — 50th percentile (median). The midpoint — half of children are above, half below. This is the typical reference value.
  • P85 — 85th percentile. 85% of children are below this line. Upper end of normal range.
  • P97 — 97th percentile. Only 3% of children exceed this. Values above may indicate overweight or above-average growth — discuss with a pediatrician if sustained.
